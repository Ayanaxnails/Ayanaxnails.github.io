Good to know

Before you come

Appointment policy

A $20 Zelle deposit holds your spot and comes off your total. It is due within 24 hours of booking, or the time is released. Deposits are non-refundable. Please be on time. No extra guests. If you are more than 10 minutes late or don't show, your appointment is canceled and the deposit is kept.

How do I pay?

Zelle or cash only. The $20 deposit is sent by Zelle after you DM your inspo, with your Instagram @ in the memo. The rest is paid at your appointment.

Where are you located?

Riverside, CA. The exact address is sent the day of your appointment.

Will I get a reminder?

Yes. You get a text to confirm when you book, and a reminder before your appointment.

What if I cancel?

If you cancel, your deposit is kept. To book again after a cancellation, a $30 deposit is required, and it is kept if you cancel again.
